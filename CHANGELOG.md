# Bagnon Consolidator Release Notes

## v1.0.3

### Features
* **WoW Forever Support:** Added client compatibility and TOC packaging directives for WoW Forever (Camelot, interface 16001).
* **Modern Expansion Sync:** Updated Retail interface version to 120100 (Midnight 12.1).
* **Library Update:** Updated LibItemMove-1.0 submodule to e5390f8 for WoW Forever support.

## v1.0.2

### Bugfixes
* **Icon Resolution:** Corrected consolidate button icon FileDataID from Mana Tap (`135738`) to Charge Positive (`135769`).

## v1.0.1

### Improvements
* **Library Update:** Updated LibItemMove-1.0 submodule with modernized container resolution for Retail 11.0/12.0 and retired client cleanups.

## v1.0.0

### Features
* **Automatic Item Consolidation:** Consolidates matching item stacks from your backpack into personal and guild banks with a single click.
* **Smart Memory & Snapshots:** Remembers personal bank storage history per character and tracks guild bank tab mappings.
* **Conflict & Overlap Protection:** Prevents accidental movement when items exist across multiple guild tabs or both personal and guild banks.
* **Mappings Viewer:** In-game UI frame to search, inspect, and manage remembered item destinations and ignored items.
* **Multi-Language Support:** Fully localized for English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Korean, Portuguese, and Italian.
* **Multi-Expansion Compatibility:** Supports Retail, Mists of Pandaria Classic, and Classic Era.
